What no other app does
🧠

Collective memory

Two DJs with the same track —different file, format or tags— share cues, beat grid and corrections: the app matches them by how they sound, not by name. Where several DJs agree, you adopt their cues in one click.

🔄

Round-trip with your DJ software

Bring your cues, loops, beat grid and playlists in from Rekordbox, Traktor and VirtualDJ, and send them back the same way. Tested against the real programs, not just against ourselves.

🎙

Listen

What's playing? Your phone identifies it and tells you whether it's already in your library, with its BPM and key, and what you have that mixes with it.

👥

Duplicates by sound

Finds the same track in two different files —the FLAC you bought and your old MP3— and suggests which one to keep without losing your cues.

🎶

Mixes kept apart

An hour-long recorded mix doesn't sneak into your library as a track: it's set aside on its own, plays with its waveform and, on Mac, gets its tracklist.

📂

Watched folder

Pick your downloads folder and whatever lands in it joins your library on its own, analyzed and in its folder.

Features
Everything you need for the perfect session

Professional audio analysis with cutting-edge technology.

🎵

BPM, Key & Energy

Automatic BPM, key, Camelot notation and energy level (1-10) detection. Sources: audio analysis, your tags, what you bring from Rekordbox, Traktor or VirtualDJ, and Discogs and MusicBrainz for genre.

🎨

Camelot Wheel

Interactive Camelot wheel for harmonic mixing. Instantly visualize track compatibility and plan perfect transitions.

🎧

Spectral Waveform

Rekordbox-style waveform with 3-band FFT analysis. 12,000 frames of resolution with spectral colors by frequency.

🚀

Flow Designer

Visually plan your sessions. Drag tracks, view energy curves, evaluate transitions and export to Rekordbox, Traktor or VirtualDJ.

🔥

Smart Collections

Dynamic playlists with rules: BPM, key, energy, genre, track type. Presets like "Peak Time Bangers" ready to use.

☁

Cloud Sync

Sync your library between desktop and mobile. Link devices with a 6-character code. Analyze on PC, take your data to the venue on your phone.
