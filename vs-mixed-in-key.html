Mixed In Key

DJ Analyzer vs Mixed In Key

Updated on November 12, 2026. Mixed In Key's side comes from the press (link at the end); its price and feature list are on its website. If something has changed, email us and we'll fix it.

Mixed In Key is the reference for getting the key of your tracks. DJ Analyzer gets it too, but its ground is a different one: identifying what's playing, sharing what's known about each track with other DJs, and preparing your set.

Mixed In Key

It analyzes your files on the computer: the key in Camelot notation, energy from 1 to 10, and automatic cue points for your DJ software.

DJ Analyzer

It gets the BPM, key (Camelot), energy, genre and track type without touching your files, and adds what only a network of DJs and a phone can give: identifying what's playing, the tracklist of a mix and the shared memory.

Where Mixed In Key wins

  • Key is its thing, and has been for many years: many DJs use it as their reference.
  • Automatic cue points on every track.
  • It writes the results into your files: any program reads them without importing anything.

Where DJ Analyzer wins

  • It identifies what's playing in a club or on the radio and tells you whether you already have it, with its BPM and key if someone has analyzed it.
  • Your recorded mixes: they play in the app and, on the Mac, get their tracklist.
  • The shared memory: what other DJs know about your track reaches you even if you have another file or format.
  • The phone: it analyzes and identifies without a computer.
  • Preparing the set: Suggest, Flow and the Camelot wheel.
  • It doesn't touch your files, and it's free today.

Rekordbox isn't the absolute truth, and we haven't measured Mixed In Key on those files. If you bring in your Rekordbox, Traktor or VirtualDJ collection, DJ Analyzer keeps the key you already had.

What if I use both?

They don't get in each other's way: DJ Analyzer doesn't write to your files, so what Mixed In Key leaves in them stays there, and your DJ software reads it as always.

DJ Analyzer is free on Windows, macOS, iPhone and Android.
