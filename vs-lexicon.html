Dónde gana Lexicon

  • Si usas varios programas de DJ, sobre todo Serato o Engine DJ, y quieres que la biblioteca sea la misma en todos sin exportar a mano.
  • El USB del CDJ sin pasar por Rekordbox (en beta).
  • Cues automáticos calculados para cada tema.
  • Escribir en tus ficheros las etiquetas que arreglas, y moverlos y renombrarlos.
  • La nube y el streaming.

Dónde gana DJ Analyzer

  • Identifica lo que suena: en un club o en la radio, Escuchar te dice qué tema es y si ya lo tienes, con su BPM y su tonalidad si alguien lo ha analizado. Y Grabar set apunta la lista de temas mientras suena.
  • Tus mezclas grabadas no entran como un tema: van aparte, suenan en la app y, en el Mac, sacan su lista de temas.
  • La memoria compartida: si otro DJ tiene tu tema, aunque sea otro fichero u otro formato, te llega lo que sabe de él.
  • Un móvil que funciona solo, gratis, sin depender del ordenador ni de la misma wifi.
  • Preparar la sesión: Suggest, Flow y la rueda de Camelot.
  • El precio: hoy, gratis.

¿Y si uso los dos?

No se pisan. DJ Analyzer exporta tu biblioteca a Rekordbox, Traktor y VirtualDJ con sus hot cues, loops y rejillas; desde ahí, Lexicon puede llevarla a los programas que DJ Analyzer no cubre.

DJ Analyzer es gratis en Windows, macOS, iPhone y Android.

DJ Analyzer vs Lexicon DJ

Updated on November 12, 2026. Lexicon's side comes from its website, pricing and manual (links at the end). If something has changed, email us and we'll fix it.

Both are for DJs who keep their music on a computer, but they don't do the same thing. This page says what each one does, where each one wins, and when it makes sense to use both.

Lexicon

A library manager and converter: it moves your collection between Rekordbox, Serato, Traktor, VirtualDJ, Engine DJ, djay Pro and Apple Music, and keeps it in sync.

DJ Analyzer

It analyzes your music, identifies what's playing and helps you prepare your set with what other DJs know about the same track. And it takes it to Rekordbox, Traktor and VirtualDJ.

Where Lexicon wins

  • If you use several DJ programs, especially Serato or Engine DJ, and want the same library in all of them without exporting by hand.
  • The CDJ USB without Rekordbox (in beta).
  • Automatic cues computed for each track.
  • Writing the tags you fix into your files, and moving and renaming them.
  • Cloud and streaming.

Where DJ Analyzer wins

  • It identifies what's playing: in a club or on the radio, Listen tells you which track it is and whether you already have it, with its BPM and key if someone has analyzed it. And Record a set writes down the tracklist while it plays.
  • Your recorded mixes don't come in as a track: they stay apart, play in the app and, on the Mac, get their tracklist.
  • The shared memory: if another DJ has your track, even as another file or format, what they know about it reaches you.
  • A phone app that works on its own, free, without depending on the computer or the same Wi-Fi.
  • Preparing the set: Suggest, Flow and the Camelot wheel.
  • The price: free today.

What if I use both?

They don't get in each other's way. DJ Analyzer exports your library to Rekordbox, Traktor and VirtualDJ with its hot cues, loops and beat grids; from there, Lexicon can take it to the programs DJ Analyzer doesn't cover.

DJ Analyzer is free on Windows, macOS, iPhone and Android.
